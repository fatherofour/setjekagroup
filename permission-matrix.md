# Who can see and do what: the permission matrix

For Setjeka Group to review, as at 4 November 2026 (item 8.5, owed by us). It describes how the platform works today. The questions at the end are the points where a decision from Setjeka would change it.

Access is decided in two layers:

1. **The matrix** gives each role a set of rights in each area of a project. An administrator can change it under **Administration → Permissions**; a change applies to every project.
2. **Rules in each area** then narrow those rights to particular records. For example, a contractor sees only their own firm's work, and only the client approves money. These rules can't be switched off in the matrix.

Someone holds a role per project, so the same person can be the architect on one project and absent from another.

---

## 1. The roles

| Group | Roles | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Setjeka team** | Development manager, project manager, planner, procurement manager, site manager, QA/QC manager, HSE manager | Setjeka's own delivery team. Setjeka's administrators (internal accounts) have the same rights on every project. |
| **Design consultants** | Architect; structural, civil, electrical and mechanical engineers | The appointed professional team |
| **Quantity surveyor** | Quantity surveyor | A consultant, with the commercial work on top |
| **Contractor** | Contractor | A contractor's or supplier's staff, linked to their firm |
| **Client** | Client | The client or developer |
| **Other** | Other | Anyone else invited to look |

## 3. The rules within each area

### Money and the client's decisions
- **Only the client approves:** money (variations, payment certificates), Stage 1 documents and stage moves. Setjeka never approves on the client's behalf (Meeting 3).
- **Payment certificates:** the QS prepares the valuation, the contract's consultant reviews it, and Setjeka's PM certifies it. The client then approves payment.
- **Contractors and design consultants** see nothing commercial.
- **Retention and the final valuation** follow the completion certificates. No one can release them early.

### What the client sees
- **Documents:** only the documents and photos Setjeka has shared with the client.
- **Delay claims:** only decided extension-of-time claims, and the revised completion dates.
- **Defects:** every defect; the client can also report defects.
- **Handover:** handover documents once they are handed over.
- **None of the site records:** NCRs, tests, hold points, hidden works, interfaces, incidents, permits, the diary, the look-ahead and the plant register.

### What a contractor sees and does
- **Their own firm's work only:**
  - their own firm's tenders, quotes and purchase orders;
  - their NCRs, tests, hidden works, incidents, permits, plant and look-ahead activities.
- **The look-ahead and plant:** they record progress only on their own activities, and add and update only their own plant.
- **Documents:** they remove only documents they added, and no folders (fixed on 4 November 2026).
- **Instructions and letters:** they write to Setjeka only.

### Who issues and signs off on site
| What | Who |
|---|---|
| Hold points and hidden works | Released or signed off by the named consultant or Setjeka |
| Permits to work | Issued, suspended and closed by the HSE manager, the site manager, the PM or the DM |
| Incidents | Investigated and closed by the HSE manager, the PM or the DM; anyone on site may report one |
| The site diary | Written and signed by the site manager, the PM or the DM; read by the site team and consultants |
| Progress on the look-ahead | Recorded by the site manager, the PM, the DM or the planner on any activity; by a contractor on their own |
| The plant register | Kept by the site manager, the PM, the DM or the HSE manager; by a contractor for their own plant |

### Instructions
- The client asks Setjeka. Setjeka's PM or DM issues instructions.
- The architect drafts contract instructions, which Setjeka issues. The client never instructs the architect directly.
- Nothing is deleted; an instruction or letter is cancelled.

### Removing things
- Setjeka removes any document or folder.
- Outside parties remove only their own documents and photos.
- Removed documents are kept in the background, with who removed them and when.

## 4. Please decide

1. **Can a site manager or HSE manager be someone else's employee?** Today the site manager, QA/QC, HSE, planner and procurement roles are part of the Setjeka team, with full rights. If a contractor's site manager is invited in the site manager role, they would see and change everything. We suggest adding them as **Contractor** instead, or adding a separate contractor's site manager role.
   - **Since 11 Nov:** outside parties never see Commercial unless they are the client or the QS on that project, whatever the matrix says (critique M-01). Their other rights still follow the matrix.
2. **Tenders and quotes:** should the client see every tender, every contractor's quote and every purchase order? Today they can, from tender issue onwards. Alternatives: the client sees only the tender report, or sees quotes only after award.
3. **The risk register:** should contractors and the client read all project risks? Today they can, though they can't change them. Risks often name a contractor's performance or the client's own decisions.
4. **Removing commercial records:** the QS can remove commercial records, such as a draft bill or valuation. Should removal be Setjeka's only?
5. **The "Other" role:** it can view everything except commercial, including the site records the client doesn't see. Who should it be used for, such as a funder, a tenant or an auditor? Should it see less?
6. **A changed setting.** In our test database, someone has allowed the contractor role to remove documents in the matrix (the default doesn't). The platform now blocks contractors from removing others' documents either way. Should the setting go back to the default?
